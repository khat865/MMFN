01 / Method

A collaborative generation loop.

Specialized agents preserve event anchors, construct a counter-narrative, create supporting visuals, and review the result.

02 / Findings

Detection is not solved.
Reasoning alone is not enough.

The evaluation goes beyond a final true/fake label to examine the evidence models use—and where it diverges from human judgment.

01

Human–model gap

Most evaluated MLLMs remain substantially behind expert human judgments on multimodal news veracity.

02

Visual authenticity

Models may align images with text while still failing to determine whether the visual itself is authentic.

03

Uneven CoT effects

Structured reasoning improves many models but can also amplify model-specific bias and shift decision boundaries.

How to apply

A short application.
A human review.

  1. Sign in to Hugging Face.Open the gated repository using your own account and select the access request option. Hugging Face shares your username and account email with the reviewers.
  2. Tell us who you are and your intended use.Provide your full name, university or institution, institutional email, a public university profile or ORCID link, and a brief description of your intended research use. A detailed research proposal is not required.
  3. Accept the conditions and submit.Hugging Face records your request and sends an email notification to the reviewers. If a notification is delayed, your request remains in the review queue. Reviewers may contact your institutional email to confirm your affiliation.
  4. Return to the same repository.Use the same Hugging Face account to check your request status and, once approved, download the released data and code. Submitting an application does not itself grant access.

Identity and affiliation are checked manually. Providing an email address or profile link does not by itself verify either.

Release scope & reproducibility

Synthetic resources, with a clear boundary.

The Hugging Face release contains synthetic research material, construction and evaluation code, prompts, and supporting documentation. It excludes original true-news articles, original source images, and true-post texts, including copies embedded in construction records.

The paper evaluates both true and fake news. The synthetic-only release cannot independently reproduce the full two-class results. Exact reproduction requires the original evaluation inputs and protocol; retrieving an article from its source URL does not guarantee reconstruction of the same true-post text. Source metadata supports independent acquisition under the source terms, but it is not a substitute for those withheld inputs. Report results on the released subset as subset results.
